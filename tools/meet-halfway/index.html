        How this works

        About 1,100 points around the two places are routed by car with the free OSRM demo server, which uses OpenStreetMap roads. Times assume free-flowing traffic (no rush hour, no roadworks) and OSRM's own road speeds, so they tend to run a little longer than Google or Waze on motorways. Points with no road nearby (sea, open moorland) are left unshaded. Tap anywhere on the map for exact times to that spot.

        Nice places are towns, villages and parks that have a travel guide on Wikivoyage, searched around the fairest part of the map and ranked by the longer of the two drives. Places with fuller guides get a small head start (up to 15 minutes). Place search uses Nominatim. Results are cached on this device so a repeat visit is instant.
